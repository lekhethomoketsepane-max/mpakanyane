Know about Thaba-Bosiu services

Cultural and Educational Services

Guided Plateau Walks and Tours:
Historical walks up the Thaba-Bosiu sandstone plateau and guided 1-hour tours of the cultural village and museum.

Ancestral Storytelling: Oral history sessions and legends of King Moshoeshoe I shared around a central hearth inside traditional roundavels.

On-Site Historical Museum: Exhibits and artifacts chronicling the history, heritage and origins of the Basotho nation.

Know All about Maletsunyane Services

Adventure Activities

Abseiling: Descend the 192-meter sheer rock face alongside the waterfall with licensed operators, equipment, and guide training.

Basotho Pony Trekking: Ride local ponies through the rugged highland terrain and mountain paths.

Hiking and Gorge Trails: Take guided or unguided walks into the Maletsunyane Gorge and surrounding plateau.

Mountain Biking and 4x4 Routes: Explore rough tracks and remote trails around Semonkong.

Cultural and Guided Tours

Basotho Cultural Experiences: Learn about local folklore, ancestral traditions, blanket presentations, and community history.

Birdwatching and Photography: Spot endemic species like the Lesotho rock-lark and capture panoramic landscape views.

Maletsunyane Braai Festival: Attend Africa's high-altitude annual outdoor music and braai event featuring live artists, camping, and VIP decks.

Know All About Tshehlanyane National Park

Outdoor & Adventure Activities

Guided and Self-Guided Hiking: Trails range from gentle walks to the 16km Circular Trail and the path to Lets'a-le-ts'o (Black Lake) and Matsa Mararo Falls.

Horse Riding: Basotho pony treks and guided horseback trails through the rugged mountain terrain.

Swimming: Natural rock pools and crystal-clear mountain pools along the Ts'ehlanyane and Holomo rivers.

Fly Fishing & Trout Fishing: River fishing opportunities in the park's cold basalt streams.

Birdwatching & Eco-Tours: Specialized guided walks to view endemic species and rare alpine flora.

Mountain Biking: Off-road cycling through designated highland tracks.
